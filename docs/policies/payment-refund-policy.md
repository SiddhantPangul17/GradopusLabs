# Gradopus Labs Payment, Refund & Stipend Policy

- **Version:** 1.1
- **Effective date:** To be set after legal review

- **Operated by:** Gradopus Labs, a sole proprietorship based in Maharashtra, India
- **Payments contact:** <support.gradopuslabs@gmail.com>

This policy explains what you pay to join a Gradopus Labs program, how payment works, when money is returned, and
the position on stipends.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy applies to every payment made to Gradopus Labs for a program, and to any stipend Gradopus Labs may offer
in future. It is read together with the [Student Terms & Conditions](./terms-and-conditions.md) and the
[Internship Policy](./internship-policy.md).

Gradopus Labs is an India-based brand offering project-based software development internships and technology
learning. It is not a university, college, government body or accredited institution, and it is not incorporated as
a company.

## 2. Summary

- You pay an **enrollment fee** to join a program. The amount is shown on the program's page before you enroll.
- The enrollment fee is the **only** charge. There is **no** application fee, registration fee, assessment fee or
  certificate fee.
- Paying the fee gives you access to the program. It does **not** guarantee that your work will be accepted, that
  you will complete the program, or that you will receive a certificate, a job or a placement.
- Enrollment fees are **not refundable**, except in the cases listed in section 8, such as duplicate payments or a
  program cancelled by Gradopus Labs.
- Gradopus Labs does **not** currently pay stipends.

## 3. Payment terms are program-specific

Each program has its own fee, shown on its page. Programs can differ in price, duration and content. Do not assume
that one program's fee or terms apply to another.

Gradopus Labs may waive the fee for some students, for example through a scholarship. Waivers are given in writing
and do not change the program's requirements. `[TO BE CONFIRMED: whether fee waivers or scholarships will be
offered, and how]`

## 4. How fees are disclosed

Before you pay, you will see:

- on the **program page**: the program fee, duration, weekly time commitment, what is included, the learning
  outcomes and the completion requirements;
- on the **checkout page**: the program, the amount payable, and links to this policy and the Student Terms &
  Conditions.

The amount charged is always the program fee stored by Gradopus Labs at the time you pay. You cannot be charged a
different amount at checkout, and no fee, tax or charge will be added that was not shown to you before you paid.

`[TO BE CONFIRMED: the fee for each program, and whether prices include GST]`

We will not use false urgency, such as fake countdowns or invented "limited seats", to pressure you into paying.

## 5. What is charged and what is not

- **Applying or registering an account:** free.
- **Joining a program:** the program's enrollment fee.
- **Assessment and reviews:** included in the enrollment fee; no extra charge.
- **Resubmissions within the attempt limit:** included; no extra charge.
- **Certificate of Completion:** free. There is no fee to issue, download, verify or correct a certificate.
- **Other charges:** none.

If Gradopus Labs ever introduces any other charge, it will apply only to students who agree to it in writing before
they enroll. It will never be a condition of receiving a certificate for a program you have already joined.

## 6. Stipends

Gradopus Labs does **not** currently pay stipends for any program.

If a stipend is offered for a program in future, the internship details will state in writing, before you accept a
place:

- the stipend amount or how it is calculated;
- who is eligible and any documented conditions;
- how often and on what dates it will be paid, and how;
- any deductions, which will be made only where the law allows or requires them;
- what to do if a payment is late or disputed;
- whether a separate written agreement is needed.

A stipend will never be used to avoid obligations that apply under labour law.

## 7. Payment methods and receipts

- Online payments are processed by **Razorpay**, a third-party payment provider. You can pay using the methods
  Razorpay's checkout offers, such as UPI, cards, net banking or wallets.
  `[TO BE CONFIRMED: Razorpay live account approval; online payment is not available until it is approved]`
- You enter your payment details **directly into Razorpay's checkout**. Gradopus Labs does not receive or store your
  card number, CVV, UPI ID or bank details.
- Gradopus Labs will **never** ask for your UPI PIN, card PIN, OTP, net-banking password or any other payment
  credential, by phone, email, chat or any other way. If anyone asks for these claiming to be from Gradopus Labs, do
  not share them, and report it to us.
- **Only pay through the official checkout on the Gradopus Labs website.** We do not ask students to pay by
  personal bank transfer or to a personal UPI ID.
- After a successful payment you receive:
  - access to the program as soon as the payment is confirmed;
  - a record in the **Payments** section of your student dashboard;
  - a payment confirmation email, if email is set up, showing the program, amount, payment reference and date.

The payment confirmation is **not a tax invoice**. `[TO BE CONFIRMED: whether tax invoices will be issued, see
section 14]`

## 8. Refunds

**Enrollment fees are not refundable**, including if you withdraw, do not complete the program, or are not satisfied
with your own results, **except** in the following cases, where your money will be returned:

1. **Duplicate payment:** you were charged more than once for the same enrollment.
2. **Failed payment that was debited:** money left your account but the payment did not complete and you did not
   receive access. Banks and Razorpay often reverse these automatically.
3. **Program unavailable:** your payment was confirmed but the program had already been withdrawn, so you did not
   receive access.
4. **Program cancelled by Gradopus Labs:** we cancel a program you paid for before you complete it (section 10).
5. **Significant change by Gradopus Labs:** we make a significant change to a program you paid for that materially
   reduces what you were promised, and you choose to withdraw (section 10).
6. **Any other case where the law requires a refund.**

Nothing in this policy limits any right to a refund or remedy that you have under applicable law.

**How to request a refund:** email <support.gradopuslabs@gmail.com> from the email address on your
account, with:

- your name;
- the program;
- the payment date and the payment reference (shown in your dashboard and the confirmation email);
- the reason for your request.

Refunds are paid to the **original payment method**. We will tell you in writing whether your request is accepted and
why.

## 9. Cancellation and withdrawal by you

- You can withdraw from a program at any time by telling us in writing (see the Internship Policy, section 17).
- Withdrawing does not lead to a refund, except in the cases in section 8.
- After withdrawing, your completed reviews stay on your record, and a certificate is issued only if you met all of
  the completion requirements before you withdrew.

## 10. Program cancellation or significant changes by Gradopus Labs

- If we **cancel** a program you have paid for before you complete it, we will tell you as early as possible and
  refund your enrollment fee in full. `[TO BE CONFIRMED: whether a partial refund applies if most of the program has
  already been delivered]`
- If we make a **significant change** that materially reduces what was promised, such as removing major parts of the
  program or substantially changing its duration, we will tell you before the change takes effect. You may then
  continue, or withdraw and receive a refund.
- Minor changes that keep the program's learning outcomes, such as updating a task or correcting materials, are not
  significant changes.
- If a program ends early for reasons outside your control, you will not lose a certificate you have already earned.

## 12. Disputed and duplicate payments

- The website is designed to prevent duplicate orders: while one payment for a program is in progress, starting
  checkout again resumes the same order.
- If you think you were charged twice, or charged the wrong amount, contact us with the payment references before
  raising a dispute with your bank, so we can resolve it quickly. You keep any right you have to raise a dispute with
  your bank or payment provider.
- If you raise a chargeback or payment dispute, we will work with you and Razorpay to resolve it.
  `[TO BE CONFIRMED: whether program access is paused while a chargeback is open]` We will **not** revoke a
  certificate you have already earned because of a payment dispute.

## 13. Failed or delayed payments

- If a payment fails, you are not enrolled and you can try again.
- Some payments, especially UPI, can take time to confirm. Until Razorpay confirms the payment to our website, your
  enrollment is shown as **awaiting confirmation**. You can check the status on the checkout page.
- Access is given only after the payment is confirmed by Razorpay to our server. A success message in your banking
  app alone does not activate enrollment.
- If money has left your account but your enrollment is not confirmed after `[TO BE CONFIRMED: time]`, contact us
  with the payment reference.

Students pay an enrollment fee per program; no certificate fee; no stipends for now; no refunds, with lawful

### Implementation facts used

Razorpay is the only provider and is off until configured and approved; it has been tested only against a simulated

invoice". Refunds are not implemented (`refund-service.ts` always refuses), and refunds made in the Razorpay dashboard
are not reflected in the platform. Payments confirmed for an unavailable program are flagged `program_unavailable`.
No GST or tax handling exists.
