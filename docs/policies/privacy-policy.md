# Gradopus Labs Privacy Policy

- **Version:** 1.3

- **Privacy contact:** <support.gradopuslabs@gmail.com>

This policy explains what personal data Gradopus Labs collects through its website and programs, why, who can see
it, what is public, how long it is kept and what you can ask us to do. It describes how the website works today.
Where something is not yet in place, the policy says so.

## 1. Who we are

Gradopus Labs is an India-based brand offering practical, project-based software development internships and
technology learning. It is not a university, college, government body or accredited institution, and it is not
incorporated as a company.

Gradopus Labs is a sole proprietorship based in Maharashtra, India. Its proprietor decides how and why
personal data is processed for Gradopus Labs. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Gradopus Labs and its operator, and
"you" means anyone who uses our website or takes part in our programs.

**How to contact us about privacy:** email <support.gradopuslabs@gmail.com>, or use the contact form on our
website.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers personal data we process when you:

- visit the Gradopus Labs website;
- create an account and sign in;
- enrol in, pay for and take part in a program;
- submit work for review;
- receive, download or have someone verify a certificate;
- contact us.

It does not cover other websites or services you use during a program, such as GitHub or a hosting provider. They
have their own privacy policies (see section 19).

## 3. Summary

- We collect your **name, email address and password** when you create an account. Your password is stored only as
  a secure hash.
- We record your **enrolments, payments, progress and submitted work**, and the **feedback** reviewers give you.
- We do **not** ask for your phone number, date of birth, address, college details, identity documents, or
  information about your religion, caste, health or finances.
- **Certificates** can be verified publicly. A search by certificate ID shows your **initials**; the link in a
  certificate's QR code shows your **full name** (section 10).
- We use only the **cookies needed to keep you signed in** securely. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.
- We do **not** sell your personal data or send marketing emails.
- Payments are handled by **Razorpay**, and emails are sent by **Resend**, when those services are switched on.

## 4. Personal data we collect

### 4.1 Account information

When you register, we collect:

- your **full name**, which also appears on any certificate you earn;
- your **email address**, used to sign in and to contact you;
- your **password**, which is stored only as a one-way hash (Argon2id) and never in readable form.

We also record your account role (student or administrator), your account status (for example active or
suspended), when you accepted our terms, when you last signed in, and when your account was created and updated.

We do not currently verify email addresses. You can change your name on your profile page; to change your email
address, contact us (section 14).

### 4.2 Applications and educational information

The website does not currently have an application form, and it does not ask for educational information such as
your college, course or year of study.

All programs are currently open enrolment, so no application information is collected. If we ever introduce
applications, this policy will be updated first to say exactly what will be asked and why.

### 4.3 Enrolments and payments

- **Enrolments:** the programs you join, enrolment status and dates, how you enrolled (online payment, or a manual
  enrolment by our team) and the amount paid.
- **Payments:** the amount and currency, the payment status, Razorpay's order and payment references, any failure
  code, and timestamps.
- We **do not** receive or store your card number, CVV, UPI ID, UPI PIN, bank details or OTPs. You enter those
  directly into Razorpay's checkout.

### 4.4 Learning progress, submissions and assessment records

- which modules you have marked as read and your progress through each program;
- each submission attempt: **text answers** (up to 10,000 characters) or a **project link** (for example a GitHub
  repository or a live deployment URL), and when you submitted it;
- review results: the outcome ("Accepted", "Changes requested" or "Rejected"), the reviewer's written feedback, any
  score and when it was reviewed.

All attempts and their feedback are kept, so you and your reviewer can see the history of your work. File uploads
are not available on the website.

We never ask for your GitHub password or the password of any other external account. If a task asks you to give
a reviewer access to a private repository, you do this through the code host's own sharing settings (for example
GitHub's collaborator invitations). We will then see the repository and your account name on that code host.

### 4.5 Certificate records

For each certificate: the certificate ID, your name and the program title as they were when it was issued, the
completion and issue dates, the certificate's status (valid, revoked or replaced), who issued it, and any
correction or revocation details, including the reason. A verification code is stored in protected form so that the
certificate's QR code can be checked.

### 4.6 Notifications and emails

- **In-app notifications** about your account and program, such as review results and certificates.
- **Email records:** the type of email, its status and the email provider's message reference. The email address
  itself is looked up when the email is sent, not stored with the record.

### 4.7 Messages you send us

When you use the contact form, we collect your **name, email address, subject and message**. The message is sent by
email (through Resend, see section 11) to our support inbox, with your email address set as the reply address so we
can answer you. It is **not stored on the website**.

Messages that reach our support inbox, whether through the form or by email, are kept for 1 year after the matter
is resolved (section 13).

### 4.8 Technical information

- **Security and rate limiting:** to stop abuse such as password guessing or contact-form spam, the website counts
  requests from each IP address and, for sign-in and password reset, each email address. These counts are stored
  only as a one-way hash and are deleted automatically within about one hour.
- **Records of administrative actions:** we keep an internal record of significant actions taken by our team, such
  as reviewing work, issuing or revoking a certificate, or suspending an account. These records identify the team
  member and the affected record. They do not include IP addresses or browser details.
- **Hosting:** the company that hosts our website may automatically record technical information about requests,
  such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. `[TO BE CONFIRMED: hosting provider and what request
  logs it keeps, and for how long]`
- **Cookies:** see section 16.

## 10. How certificate verification works

When we issue a certificate, it gets a **certificate ID** (for example GL-2026-000001) and a **QR code** linking to a
private verification page. Anyone can check a certificate in two ways:

1. **By certificate ID** on the website's verification page. This shows:
   - the certificate ID and its status;
   - for a valid certificate: the holder's **initials only**, the program title and the completion and issue dates;
   - for a revoked certificate: the program title and the date it was revoked;
   - for a replaced certificate: the program title, the date it was replaced and, where the replacement is valid,
     the replacement's certificate ID.
2. **By the QR code link** printed on the certificate. This shows the same details, but with the holder's **full
   name**. This page asks search engines not to index it. Only people who have the certificate or its link can
   open it.

Verification **never** shows your email address, account details, submissions, feedback, scores, payment details
or the reason for a revocation or correction. We do not keep a record of who checks a certificate. Requests are
counted only for rate limiting (section 4.8).

Certificate IDs follow a predictable pattern, so anyone could try IDs on the verification page. This is why a
search by ID shows only initials.

The certificate PDF itself is not public. Only you, while signed in, and our team can download it.

More detail is in the Certificate Issuance & Verification Policy.

## 13. How long we keep personal data

Some data is deleted automatically today:

- **Password reset links:** expire after 30 minutes and are then deleted.
- **Rate-limiting counts:** deleted within about one hour.
- **Email records:** deleted 180 days after they are created.
- **In-app notifications:** deleted 365 days after they are created.

For everything else, we keep personal data for these periods:

- **Your account, enrolments, progress, submissions and feedback:** while your account is active, and then for
  **1 year after you last sign in**. After that, they are deleted.
- **Certificate records:** with **no fixed end**, so that anyone checking a certificate keeps getting an accurate
  answer. Your certificate stays verifiable even after your account is deleted, but you will no longer be able to
  download the PDF, so please keep a copy.
- **Payment records:** for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
- **Emails and messages you send us:** **1 year after the matter is resolved**.
- **Records of administrative actions:** **3 years**.

We may keep a record for longer where it is needed to handle an open dispute, to prevent fraud or to keep our
platforms secure, and only for as long as that need lasts.

Deleting inactive accounts and old records is currently done by our team, not automatically.

## 16. Cookies and tracking

The website uses only the cookies needed for signing in securely:

- **Session cookie** (`authjs.session-token`): keeps you signed in, for up to 7 days.
- **Security cookie** (`authjs.csrf-token`): protects sign-in forms against cross-site request forgery.
- **Redirect cookie** (`authjs.callback-url`): remembers where to send you after signing in.

These cookies cannot be read by scripts on the page, and they are needed for the website to work, so there is no
option to turn them off while signed in.

We do **not** use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking cookies, and the website does not use other
browser storage. When you choose to pay, Razorpay's checkout script is loaded from Razorpay and may use its own
cookies or similar technologies under Razorpay's privacy policy.

If we add analytics or any other tracking in future, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where the
law requires it.
