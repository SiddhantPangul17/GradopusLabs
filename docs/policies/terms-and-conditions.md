## 1. About these terms

These Student Terms & Conditions ("**Terms**") are the general rules for taking part in Gradopus Labs programs,
including project-based internships and technology learning programs.

Gradopus Labs is an India-based brand offering practical, project-based software development internships and
technology learning. It is **not** a university, college, government body or accredited institution, and it is
not incorporated as a company.

In these Terms, "**we**", "**us**" and "**our**" mean Gradopus Labs and the person who operates it. "**You**"
means the student who applies for, registers for or takes part in a program.

## 2. Purpose and scope

These Terms explain what you can expect from us, what we expect from you, and what happens if something goes
wrong. They apply to:

- applying for, registering for and taking part in any Gradopus Labs program;
- using any account, website, repository or communication channel we provide for a program;

Nothing in these Terms removes or limits any right you have under applicable law, including as a consumer, as a
participant in an internship, or in relation to your personal data. If any part of these Terms appears to do so,

## 3. Definitions

- **Student**: anyone who applies for, registers for or takes part in a Gradopus Labs program.
- **Intern**: a student who has been selected for, and has accepted a place in, a Gradopus Labs internship.
- **Program**: a structured internship or learning program offered by Gradopus Labs, made up of modules, tasks
  and projects, with published completion requirements.
- **Internship details:** the written terms for a specific program, shown to you before you enrol or accept a
  place, for example on the program's page. They state things such as the enrolment fee, duration, weekly time
  commitment, tasks and completion requirements.
- **Enrolment fee:** the amount you pay to join a program, shown on the program's page.
- **Task**: an assignment within a program, with stated instructions, submission requirements and review criteria.
- **Project**: a task, or group of tasks, in which you build software and submit evidence of it, such as a GitHub
  repository, a live deployment URL, documentation or a demonstration.
- **Submission**: anything you send us for review in response to a task.
- **Mentor** or **reviewer**: a person who supports you or reviews your submissions on behalf of Gradopus Labs.
- **Certificate**: a document issued by Gradopus Labs recording that you met the published completion requirements
  of a program, as documented in our records.

## 9. Academic integrity, originality and use of AI tools

Your submissions must honestly show **your own** work and understanding.

**You must:**

- write or produce your submissions yourself, except where the task allows you to use other material;
- credit any third-party code, libraries, tutorials, articles or other resources you use, as the task requires;
- in group tasks, accurately show your own contribution;
- be able to explain the work you submit, if a reviewer asks.

**You must not:**

- copy another person's work, including another student's, and present it as your own;
- submit work written for you by someone else, whether paid or unpaid;
- share your solutions with other students so they can submit them as their own, unless the task allows it;
- falsify evidence of your work, such as commit history, screenshots, deployment URLs or demonstrations.

**AI tools.** Some tasks may allow AI coding assistants or other AI tools, and some may not. You must:

- follow each task's instructions on whether, and how, AI tools may be used;
- disclose where you have used AI tools in a submission, in the way the task asks;
- never submit AI-generated work as your own where the task does not allow it, or without disclosing it.

`[TO BE CONFIRMED: the general rule on AI-assisted work, and how disclosure should be made]`

## 15. Certificate verification, corrections and fraudulent claims

**Verification.** Each certificate has a unique certificate ID and a QR code. Anyone, such as an employer, can check
a certificate's status (valid, revoked or replaced) on our verification page, together with limited details such as
the program and dates. A search by ID shows the holder's initials; the QR code link shows the full name. Our
records, not the printed document, decide whether a certificate is genuine. See the
[Certificate Issuance & Verification Policy](./certificate-policy.md).

**Corrections.** If your certificate contains an error, such as a misspelled name or the wrong program, tell us
under section 18. If we confirm the error, we will issue a corrected certificate. The original is then marked as
replaced, and there is no charge for correcting our own errors.

**Revocation.** A certificate may be revoked if there is documented evidence that it was obtained through
plagiarised, falsified or fraudulent work, or through a serious breach of section 9. Before revoking a certificate,
we will tell you what the concern is and give you a fair opportunity to respond, and you can appeal under section
18. The process is set out in the Certificate Issuance & Verification Policy.

**Fraudulent claims.** You must not:

- create, alter or forge a Gradopus Labs certificate, or present one that was revoked or replaced as valid;
- claim a certificate, program completion, role or result that our records do not show;
- describe a certificate as a degree, government qualification, accreditation or job offer.

If someone asks us to verify a certificate, we will tell them truthfully what our records show.
